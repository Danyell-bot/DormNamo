About us

Helping students find a home near campus

DormNamo helps MSU-IIT students find boarding houses, dormitories and bedspaces in Iligan City, and helps landowners fill their rooms. We started it because finding a room meant asking around, scrolling old posts and walking door to door, often without knowing the rent until you got there.

What we believe

Three rules shape everything on the site.

How matching works

Every room gets a match score that you can always ask Namo to explain.

  1. You set what mattersBudget, walking distance and must-haves like water, Wi-Fi or a curfew. Each one can be a must-have, a nice-to-have, or not important.
  2. Rooms are checked against itA must-have a place can’t meet hides it. Anything the listing doesn’t say stays unknown and is never guessed.
  3. It learns from your swipesA small model in your own browser learns from the rooms you like and pass, so the order gets better the more you use Discover.

Meet the team

DormNamo is a student project for CCC100 by Team Gwapo, five developers who built it together.
